About Linda

Why select Linda?

  • Her programs are informed by the latest research and best practices in gratitude, positive psychology, and neuroscience
  • Her programs are designed to engage adult learners and build and broaden the applications of new knowledge
  • She emphasizes measurable leadership and culture performance strategies that improve how people work together and advance the well-being of employees and their organization

Linda’s keynotes reflect clients’ desired outcomes with pre-event planning to ensure cultural and program objectives are met. Some of Linda’s recent speaking engagements include:

  • The Science of Gratitude: The Positive Impact on Employees and the Organization
  • The Gratitude Edge: Harnessing the Science of Gratitude to Elevate Well-Being, Engagement and Partnerships
  • The Health and Well-Being Benefits of Gratitude

Thought leadership

Linda shares her subject matter expertise in a variety of ways, including:

  • Her TEDx Talk, Gratitude: The Power to Heal the World
  • Her international academic research partnerships in England and Australia
  • As a contributor to the Wharton Healthcare Quarterly feature series on gratitude since 2017
  • Author of Gratitude Heals®: A Journal for Inspiration and Guidance
  • Newly released: What Went Well Today? A 60-day e-Journal mapping character strengths, energy and mood levels, milestones and growth

Coaching

Linda is honored to coach esteemed leaders across industries, using strength-based and well-being coaching methodologies informed by current research and evidence-based practices in neuroscience, positive psychology, and gratitude, ensuring her work supports mission-driven cultures. Linda is an ICF-ACC Certified Executive Coach and a National Board-Certified Health and Well-Being Coach (NBC-HWC*). She holds certifications in Brain-Based Coaching from the NeuroLeadership Institute and in Positive Psychology Health and Well-Being through the College of Executive Coaching. She is a member of the Institute of Coaching at McLean Hospital/Harvard Medical School Associate.

*Linda maintains an active, nationally recognized credential, follows the NBC-HWC Code of Ethics and is HIPAA-compliant.
